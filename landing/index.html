How it works

Ask. It searches. You get the links.

Your assistant sends your request to viajante. viajante searches from your machine and returns the rows, the links, and the status of each search.

  • Runs on your computer
  • No API keys

Flights.

Give a route and dates. Each row comes back with its fields and a link to the search it came from.

Hotels.

Name the city, the dates, the guests and the currency. Free cancellation is the default. Ask for non-refundable stays if you want them.

No results isn't the same as none.

Every search says why it came back empty.

provider_emptyThe provider returned nothing. Only this one means none found.
filtered_outRows came back, and viajante's own filters removed them.
not_loadedThe search didn't finish. Availability is unknown.
